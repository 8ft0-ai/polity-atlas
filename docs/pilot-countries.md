# Pilot Countries

These are the countries which will be used to test Polity Atlas features during development. They are selected to provide a range of political and geographic contexts, including different continents and government types. The list is not exhaustive and may be updated as the project progresses.

## States

NZL — New Zealand

GBR — United Kingdom

CHN — China
IND — India
IDN — Indonesia
JPN — Japan


## Parliamentary pilot contract

All ten pilots use the same IPU Parline parliamentary schema and normalization semantics. Country-specific transformation branches and hand-authored parliamentary prose are not part of the contract. Differences such as bicameral structure, appointed or indirectly elected chambers, overhang seats and partial renewals must emerge from source data and the shared schema.

The pilot profiles deliberately model the most recent parliamentary election/renewal rather than attempting to maintain current party composition.
